RR-01-01-01

Operating Plan Design

Service Design & Network Planning › Operating Plan Design · confidence: high

Description

The need for an updated operating plan arises when the current plan's performance metrics indicate inefficiencies or when regulatory changes necessitate adjustments. The Service Design / Network Planning Analyst uses NetControl to analyze network performance and propose improvements. The VP Network Planning & Operations reviews these proposals, incorporating feedback from the Chairman, President and CEO. Decisions are made on whether proposed changes align with strategic goals and regulatory requirements, balancing operational efficiency against compliance risks. Once approved, the updated operating plan is integrated into Unified Plan 2020 for implementation across the network. The VP Network Planning & Operations oversees this transition. The process concludes when the new operating plan is fully implemented and initial performance metrics indicate alignment with strategic objectives and regulatory compliance.
